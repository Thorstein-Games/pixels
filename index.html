How to play

  • Tap an empty square to place a block. Tap it again to remove it.
  • Small squares? Press and hold to see where the block will go, slide to adjust, and let go to place it. Slide off the board to cancel.
  • Pixels bounce off walls and blocks. Trap them all in 15% of the board to win.
  • Place a block on a pixel and it gets stuck. You can't win until you free it.
  • Score = moves × seconds, so it climbs while you play. Like golf, lower wins: be quick and use few moves.
  • Restarting the daily resets the board, not your clock or moves.

Restart?

The board resets. Your time and moves keep counting.
